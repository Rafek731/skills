Add **`writer-reviewer-protocol`** to the **Productivity** bucket, so it ships in the Claude Code plugin, gets a docs page, and is routed by `ask-matt` as a standalone.

`writer-reviewer-protocol` (user-invoked) runs a task through a write-and-review loop. A low-effort suborchestrator has a writer produce the work and blind reviewers score it 1-10, sending it back for revision until every reviewer scores 9 or higher, a bar neither the writer nor the reviewers are told. Writer and reviewer effort, and the reviewer count, come from the user.
